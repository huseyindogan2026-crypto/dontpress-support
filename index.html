Don’t Press.

One button. 100 rounds. Read the rule before you touch anything.

Help & support

Don’t Press is a native offline iPhone game by Huseyin Dogan. For help or to report a problem, open a support issue. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Issues are public: never include passwords or personal information.

How to play

Tap and release the requested number of times. When instructed to wait, keep your finger off the button until time runs out. For hold rounds, release when the button says RELEASE. In timing rounds, press when it says NOW inside the white arc.

Game modes

Classic has 100 rounds with progressively shorter timers and tougher rules. Daily provides 20 rounds that change at midnight UTC. Practice offers 12 rounds with more time. One mistake ends the run in every mode.

Saved progress

Pause to save your completed rounds. Resume from the home screen, even after relaunching. The current round restarts. Starting a new run replaces your previous save. Winning or losing clears that run’s save; personal bests remain.

Sound or haptics

Open Settings from the home screen. Sound effects respect silent mode. Availability of haptics depends on the device.

Privacy

No account, advertising or analytics. Read the privacy policy for local storage, sharing and support details.
