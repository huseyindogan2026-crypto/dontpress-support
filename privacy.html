Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Don’t Press for iPhone

Don’t Press is an offline game developed by Huseyin Dogan. The app does not collect or send personal data to the developer. It contains no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved run (mode, random seed, cleared-round count and date), personal bests, and sound and haptic preferences in its local storage. It uses the device date to generate Daily rounds. The app does not upload this information.

Retention and deletion

Starting a new run replaces the previous saved run. Winning or losing clears that save. Daily saves expire after their UTC day. The app keeps at most 35 daily high-score entries. Other personal bests and settings remain until the app and its data are deleted. Delete the app, rather than offloading it, to remove its local data. iOS backups may contain app data depending on your settings. Apple controls those backups, which you can manage through iOS.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score, the iOS share sheet sends only the score text you choose to share to your selected recipient or service. That service’s privacy policy applies.

Support and this website

Opening a support or privacy link uses your browser. GitHub hosts this website and the support issue tracker. GitHub may process technical visit information under its privacy statement. If you open a support issue, its contents and your GitHub account name are public. Do not include personal or sensitive information. The developer uses support issue information only to respond to and resolve requests. You can edit or delete your issue contents using GitHub.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated here if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Huseyin Dogan through the support issue tracker, without including personal information.
